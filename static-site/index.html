Kadena Garden
Mining infrastructure

        
Open-source solo mining for Kadena: miners point their hashrate at a shared set of nodes, and every block their rigs find pays to their own wallet, with no pool holding funds. Each miner picks how they get paid: Direct, straight to their own wallet; Sweep, collected into one chain for them; or Convert, swapped into tokens such as USDC. A collaboration between Pascal (@CryptoPascal31) and Saudi.KDA (@KadenaSAUDI).

        
Built byPascal and Saudi.KDA, with the Kadena Nexus dev teammine.kadena.ws

      

      

        
KadenaTrace
Security · Fraud tracing

        
Where did the stolen crypto go? Paste a drained wallet on any of 11 networks and follow the funds hop by hop, through swaps and bridges, to the exchanges that can freeze them. A live feed of hacks, early warnings when exchange wallets drain fast, and every case and every move of the funds recorded on Kadena in the free.kadena-trace Pact contract. Began as a Kadena Devs challenge; completed by the Nexus team. Alerts at @KadenaTrace.

        
Built byKadena Nexus dev teamtrace.kadena.ws

Kadena Privacy
Zero-knowledge · Pact

        
Privacy tools for Kadena built natively in Pact with zero-knowledge proofs. First up is Prove: lock KDA in a vault and show an app that you hold at least a given amount without revealing your wallet or balance. Proof of age and shielded transfers with auditor view keys follow.

        
StatusIn developmentGoes live atprivacy.kadena.wsBuilt byKadena Nexus dev team

      

    


    
About the challenges. From January to October 2026, Kadena Devs ran public challenges with a prize for the best working build. The P2P Marketplace came out of that program, and so did the people who now make up the Kadena Nexus dev team, led by Mustafa Zahid. We are not opening new public challenges. New projects are chosen and built by the team, and the list above is kept up to date.
